    Rev E. Every part is fit-checked against Waveshare's own 3D model of the board, the speaker and the measured parts, and every heavy part is held by printed features, screws or crush ribs, never glue (only the tub's two halves are glued to each other); the wires lie loose with slack. The clearances were tuned on a Bambu P2S (PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle): on another printer, print the fit coupon first.

    The print files, each piece's pose on the bed and its colour are in the print guide.
